      A visit is one browser tab opening the site. Reloads in the same tab are not counted again, and neither are browsers that ask not to be tracked. Days are Central time. "Direct" means the visitor typed the address, used a bookmark, or came from an app that does not say where it sent them. Text message and email only count when the link ends in ?from=text or ?from=email.
